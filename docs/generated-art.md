# Crafted-item artwork

15 original AI-generated 192px replacements for the starter-catalogue results. Fine engraving, neutral light background and selective colors in liquid, food, leather, fletching and other materials. Existing result paths remain unchanged. Dangerous now depicts a weapon threatening its wielder; Imprecise artwork is also included. No recipe rules or item data changed.

## v0.1.39

Four separate framed item icons added: mortar and pestle, copper distillation still, honey and dwarf short sword. Corrected the matching demonstration item and recipe images without changing crafting rules. Existing world documents are not automatically rewritten. Seven original trade emblems remain unchanged.

## Legacy icon compatibility

Restored all 21 icon paths removed in v0.1.31: 12 unambiguous legacy filenames use the current corresponding artwork; 9 ambiguous or unmatched icons retain their original artwork. Existing recipes, world items and actor items can keep their saved image paths. No world documents are rewritten. `docs/legacy-icon-paths.json` records all 33 pre-v0.1.31 paths; QA checks that they remain available.
