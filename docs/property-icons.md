# Property icons

39 original AI-generated transparent 128px glyphs: 27 qualities and 12 flaws. Selective color highlights the meaningful detail in qualities and flaws alike. Includes all approved revisions, including the minimal plate top for Partial.

Used in all four property lists of the item forge: qualities, flaws, critical qualities and failure flaw pool. Unknown addon properties keep their labels without requesting nonexistent artwork. Labels, tooltips, selection and property levels are unchanged.

The inventory is based on the official WFRP4e source snapshot linked in the project audit; world-added properties are not included.
